Features

Everything You Need for
Seamless Communication

Powerful features designed to make your real-time conversations smooth, secure, and enjoyable.

Real-Time Messaging

Messages delivered instantly via WebSocket connections. No polling, no delays — pure real-time communication with typing indicators.

Password Protected Rooms

Every room is protected with a password. Only authorized users can join your conversations, ensuring complete privacy.

Multi-User Rooms

Invite unlimited users to any room. See who's online, track user presence, and manage audio/video status in real time.

Emoji Reactions

React to conversations with live emoji reactions. Express yourself quickly — reactions are broadcast to all room members in real time.

Secure by Design

Token-based authentication, password hashing, and encrypted WebSocket connections keep your data safe.

Live Audio & Video

Toggle your microphone and camera in real-time. Local preview shows exactly what others see, with a live audio visualizer.

Message Search

Search through chat messages with Ctrl+F. Find any message instantly with highlighted results and match counting.

Sound Notifications

Get audio alerts for new messages. Toggle sounds on/off per preference. Never miss an important message again.

Command Palette

Quick access to every feature with Ctrl+K. Navigate, search, and control the app without touching the mouse.

About Us

The Vision Behind
NEXUS Platform

We believe real-time communication should be fast, private, and accessible to everyone.

🎯

Our Mission

To provide the most intuitive and secure real-time chat platform that connects people across the globe without barriers.

👁️

Our Vision

A world where real-time communication is accessible, encrypted by default, and free from surveillance.

💡

Our Philosophy

Technology should empower, not complicate. Every feature in NEXUS is designed with simplicity at its core.

Our Journey

Phase 1

Concept & Architecture

Designed the WebSocket-based architecture, chose the tech stack, and built the foundational server.

Phase 2

Core Chat Engine

Implemented real-time messaging, room creation, user authentication with JWT, and password-protected access.

Phase 3

Rich Features

Added typing indicators, emoji reactions, audio/video toggles, user presence tracking, and system notifications.

Phase 4

v2.0 Overhaul

Command palette, toast notifications, message search, sound alerts, keyboard shortcuts, and connection quality monitoring.
